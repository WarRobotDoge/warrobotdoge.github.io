About

BookWorm Mobile is lesser known than the iOS counterpart. Despite being made for weak phones, it actually has more features. Released on November 21, 2007, BookWorm Mobile was designed to be compatible with a wide range of mobile devices, including those from manufacturers like Nokia, Samsung, and Sony. It's similar to the iOS version, but it has more features, such as a tutorial, and Lex is on the left, and not the top of the screen. The Mobile version had multiple downloads depending on screen size. The game does take on a similar look to the Palm OS BookWorm. There's only 1 video of gameplay, and I've put it below.
