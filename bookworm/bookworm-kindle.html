Feed your appetite for words with Bookworm designed for Kindle.

This popular word-puzzle game is easy to play but challenging to master. Form words by linking letters up, down and all around to level up, earn points and unlock hidden challenges. Bonus tiles heighten your score, but beware the burning letters, they could spell disaster!

Take your time to find the best word in Classic Mode or speed up your game with the fast-paced play of Action Mode. Unlock and complete themed Bonus Books when you spell certain words. Keep track of your top scores and your best and longest words in the Hall of Fame.

Play single-handedly with intuitive controls designed for Kindle.

About

BookWorm for the Amazon Kindle was released on March 27, 2012. It was sold from 2012 to about 2019. It featured a "Collections" page, and a page called "Help & Options". On the main homescreen, it appears that Lex is on a pile of books on a table, contrary to the typical library setting. The game had no color and was simply black and white. The Amazon page is still up for this game but you can't continue to buy it. There is no download for it, or any other information on it. EA had a page on BookWorm Kindle, but it wasn't updated and had very little info.
