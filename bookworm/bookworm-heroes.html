About

BookWorm Heroes was released on April 18, 2013 for iPhone and iPads. The game supported Facebook friends, which you could battle and fight each other in. The game was basically a Facebook shill. Despite it being free, it was shut down on December 1, 2014, shutting down all servers and removing it from the App Store. The letter board was quite small compared to the other games, only being a 4x4 box. The game looked relatively basic, and only a few videos of its gameplay exist.

On June 15, 2025, a BookWorm Heroes Journal was sold on eBay for $29.99. PopCap once had a BookWorm merch shop from 2003 to about 2005 (which still remains a mystery), but I couldn't find anything for sale for BookWorm Heroes in 2013, when the game was released. Perhaps an employee obtained this and just happened to sell it, nobody really knows.

Bookworm for iOS was released on March 8, 2009. The game was compact and had a look similar to BookWorm Mobile, and BookWorm for Kindle. The game was removed from the App Store on December 1, 2017. It was probably removed when Apple switched from a 32-bit architecture to a 64-bit architecture. There are very old videos online, including some gameplay.

You can download BookWorm Heroes and BookWorm for iOS above, but I'm not sure how to run them.
